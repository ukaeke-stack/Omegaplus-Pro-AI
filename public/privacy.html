Privacy Policy

Omegaplus Pro AI  |  Effective date: October 8, 2026

Omegaplus Pro AI is a sports analytics and prediction application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the service may collect, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: information needed to create and maintain an account, such as an email address and authentication credentials. Passwords are handled by the authentication system and are not displayed to administrators.
  • App activity: information associated with prediction requests, selected fixtures, prediction history, subscription/access status, and other actions needed to operate the service.
  • Support communications: feedback, suggestions, messages to administrators, and live-chat messages that a user chooses to send.
  • Technical information: limited technical information may be processed by hosting, database, security, and service providers when necessary to operate the application.

2. How we use information

Information is used to provide authentication, account management, subscriptions and access controls, sports analysis and prediction features, prediction history, customer support, fraud/security protection, troubleshooting, and service improvements.

3. Sports data and third-party services

Omegaplus Pro AI uses external sports-data and infrastructure services to provide its features. Sports fixtures, odds, statistics, and results may be obtained from third-party providers such as SportMonks and SofaScore. Hosting, database, storage, and deployment infrastructure may also process information required to operate the service. These providers may have their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to host the application, store account or support data, provide sports data, secure the service, or deliver requested functionality. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of the service and its users.

5. Data retention

Account, prediction-history, and support information may be retained while needed to provide the service, maintain records, resolve disputes, protect the service, or comply with legal obligations. Retention periods may vary according to the type of information.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. User choices and requests

Users may contact the administrator through the in-app Message Admin, Feedback, Suggestions, or Live Chat features to request help with their account or personal information. Where applicable, users may request correction or deletion of information, subject to legal and operational requirements.

8. Children's privacy

Omegaplus Pro AI is a sports analytics application and is not specifically directed to children. Users should not provide personal information belonging to a child through the service without appropriate authorization.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the in-app Message Admin or Live Chat support features in Omegaplus Pro AI.
