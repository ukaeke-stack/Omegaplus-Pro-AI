## Full description
Omegaplus Pro AI is a sports analytics and prediction application for football and basketball fans.

Use Omegaplus Pro AI to:
- Analyze upcoming football and basketball fixtures.
- View Daily Best selections and prediction confidence.
- Select fixtures by date using the calendar.
- Explore multiple leagues and prediction markets.
- Review prediction history and results.
- View Correct Score analysis for supported football fixtures.
- Review performance and settled prediction statistics.
- Build prediction selections and generate supported bookmaker booking codes.
- Send feedback, suggestions and messages to the administrator.
- Use the in-app live chat to communicate with support.

The application combines available market information with independent sports statistics and result verification to help users evaluate fixtures.

Omegaplus Pro AI is an informational and entertainment tool. Predictions, odds, statistics and other sports information are not guaranteed to be accurate, and users are responsible for their own decisions and for complying with applicable laws and regulations.
